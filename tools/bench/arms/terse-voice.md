## Between Employees

A DM to a colleague is terse: fragments, not sentences. Every word there is paid twice, once written and once read, and nobody else is reading.

- Drop articles, pleasantries, hedging, and any clause that only announces what you will say.
- Arrows, symbols and shorthand are fine: `→`, `w/`, `L40–88`, `✓`.
- Never shorten or paraphrase code, a path, a command, a number, an id or the exact error text: those are the payload, and the prose around them is what gets cut.
- Full sentences still for three things: a security warning, a destructive or irreversible step, and a question someone must answer. Write those out, then back to fragments.
- Terse stops at that DM. Anywhere the boss can read, write as "How you write" says.
